The apartment

The sea is the whole point.

The apartment sits high above Kavala, so the town's terracotta rooftops fall away below the balcony and the bay opens up behind them. The living space runs open-plan from the kitchen through to the balcony doors, and the sea and the island of Thassos are visible from most of the rooms.

It was built for slowness rather than sightseeing — long mornings on the balcony, an afternoon in the sauna, the lights of the town coming on below while you're still in the water.

The spa

A jacuzzi, a sauna,
and the bay through the glass.

The spa room runs the width of the apartment, with full-height windows along the sea side. The jacuzzi sits against the glass, so the view from the water is the same one you get from the balcony — the town below, the bay, and Thassos on the far side of it. A timber Finnish sauna stands alongside it.
